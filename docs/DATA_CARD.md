# Data card

## Sources

**Amazon Reviews 2023:** McAuley Lab, https://amazon-reviews-2023.github.io/.
The configured All_Beauty workload scans the complete category review file and chooses 4,000
catalog items by the lowest SHA256 hashes of metadata identifiers. Selection does not depend on
review labels or future popularity. Reviews are then restricted to those items. This changes
the original user-history distribution, which is explicitly reported in the audit.

Citation: Hou et al. (2024), *Bridging Language and Items for Retrieval and Recommendation*,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03952. Review text, helpful votes, metadata average ratings, rating counts,
and current prices are excluded from model features. Reviewer identifiers are hashed locally.

**MovieLens latest-small:** GroupLens, https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/latest/.
The downloadable snapshot contains 100,836 ratings, 610 users, and 9,742 catalog movies.
Download checksums identify the exact snapshot because a `latest` URL can change.
Titles and genres supply content features. Ratings are not viewing or exposure histories.

Citation: F. Maxwell Harper and Joseph A. Konstan (2015), *The MovieLens Datasets: History and
Context*, https://doi.org/10.1145/2827872. Its downloaded README permits research use with
conditions, requires acknowledgement, and requires permission for commercial or revenue-bearing
use. Research data and derived real-data model bundles are excluded from this release.

**Synthetic fixture:** deterministic category preferences generated by `data.synthetic`.
This is software-verification data. Its strong category signal cannot establish product impact.

## Task, sampling, and chronology

A positive label is a rating of at least four. The prediction task is future positive
review/rating preference. Unobserved items are unlabeled; candidate-training zeros are weak
negative assumptions rather than verified dislikes.

The configured windows use global timestamp quantiles: A 60%, B 15%, validation 10%, test 15%.
All events at or after a cutoff belong outside the earlier history. Quantile boundaries are
determined before controlled withholding. Exact duplicate events are removed; repeated ratings
at different times remain. User and item mappings are deterministic.

The catalog snapshot is assumed available throughout the experiment. TF-IDF and SVD use this
known catalog, including controlled cold items. This is transductive content availability,
not reconstruction of the catalog at historical launch dates.

Fifteen percent of items, chosen by a seeded hash, have all A/B events withheld. Withholding
affects histories, training labels, and popularity counts. Validation events may enter test
histories because they occur earlier than the test prediction cutoff. They never update model
weights or frozen aggregate features in the recorded run.

The catalog may contain naturally zero-history items in addition to the controlled cohort.
Report them separately. “Cold” does not mean a verified real product launch.

## Evaluation population

One query per eligible user predicts positives across the corresponding future window.
All pre-cutoff rated items, including low ratings, are excluded. Repeated held-out positives for
the same item count once. Users without remaining eligible positives are excluded and counted.
When configured, users are uniformly sampled with a fixed RNG seed. No users are selected by
their model performance. User-level paired bootstrap intervals quantify sampling uncertainty
conditional on this dataset and seed; they do not include model-training variance.

Macro-average metrics must be interpreted alongside returning-user counts. Both real-data
workloads contain substantial new-user populations under this global temporal protocol.

## Feedback staging

The application logs recommendation exposures separately from likes/dislikes/clicks. Feedback
must refer to an exposed item, is idempotent by event ID, and cannot originate from load-test
exposures. Export retains source, version, assignment, user hash, and timestamp.

An export is a staging dataset. It is not automatically pooled with imported ratings. Before
retraining, define a task-specific label policy, verify source/identity consistency, account for
exposure bias and delayed outcomes, set new chronological evaluation windows, and validate
quality gates. A click is not a purchase or positive review.

## Limits

No randomized historical exposure propensities are available. Therefore this project does not
estimate counterfactual business lift, train a validated contextual bandit, or equate offline
ranking improvements with conversion improvements. Current catalog text can contain temporal
information, even when explicit aggregate rating fields are excluded.
